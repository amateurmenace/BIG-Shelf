## 2. Loan Period

The loan period begins on **{{CHECKOUT_DATE}}** and the Equipment is due to be returned on or before **{{DUE_DATE}}** (the "Due Date"). Time is of the essence.

## 3. Acknowledgment of Condition

You acknowledge that you have inspected the Equipment, that it was delivered to you in good working condition, complete and undamaged except as noted at checkout, and that you have received any instructions you requested regarding its safe operation.

## 4. Permitted Use

You will use the Equipment only for lawful purposes and only for the activities for which it was borrowed. You will not: (a) permit any person other than you to use, possess, or control the Equipment; (b) rent, sublend, sell, pledge, or transfer the Equipment; (c) remove, alter, or deface any label, tag, serial number, or QR code; or (d) take the Equipment outside the United States without BIG's prior written consent.

## 5. Care, Custody, and Security

You are solely responsible for the care, custody, and security of the Equipment from the moment it leaves BIG's premises until it is returned to and accepted by BIG. You will keep the Equipment secure, protect it from weather, theft, and misuse, and use it in accordance with the manufacturer's instructions and any guidance BIG provides.

## 6. Assumption of Risk and Responsibility for Loss or Damage

**You assume all risk of, and full responsibility for, any loss, theft, disappearance of, or damage to the Equipment during the loan period, regardless of cause and regardless of fault, including loss or damage caused by accident, misuse, neglect, fire, water, weather, or the acts of any third party.** This responsibility applies whether or not you are personally at fault.

If the Equipment is lost, stolen, or damaged while in your custody, you agree to pay BIG, on demand, the full cost of repair or, if BIG determines in its reasonable discretion that the Equipment cannot be economically repaired, the full cost of replacement with equipment of comparable make, model, and condition, in each case as determined by BIG. You authorize BIG to charge any applicable repair, replacement, or late fees to you.

## 7. Late Return and Failure to Return

If you do not return the Equipment by the Due Date, you agree to pay any late fees BIG has adopted. If the Equipment is not returned within seven (7) days after the Due Date, BIG may declare the Equipment lost, treat the failure to return as conversion of BIG's property, pursue any available civil or criminal remedy, and charge you the full replacement cost. You agree to pay BIG's reasonable costs of collection, including reasonable attorneys' fees.

## 8. Release and Waiver of Liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, **you release, waive, and discharge BIG, and its officers, directors, employees, volunteers, and agents (the "Released Parties"), from any and all liability, claims, demands, or causes of action for any injury, death, property damage, or other loss arising out of or related to your possession or use of the Equipment**, whether caused by the condition of the Equipment or otherwise, except to the extent caused by the Released Parties' gross negligence or willful misconduct. You use the Equipment at your own risk.

## 9. Indemnification

**You agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Released Parties from and against any and all claims, damages, losses, liabilities, costs, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or related to your possession or use of the Equipment, your breach of this Agreement, or your violation of any law or the rights of any third party.**

## 10. No Warranty

THE EQUIPMENT IS PROVIDED "AS IS" AND "WITH ALL FAULTS." BIG MAKES NO WARRANTY OF ANY KIND, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING ANY WARRANTY OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE, AND DISCLAIMS ALL SUCH WARRANTIES TO THE FULLEST EXTENT PERMITTED BY LAW.

## 11. Insurance

BIG does not insure the Equipment while it is in your custody. Any insurance covering the Equipment during the loan period is your responsibility.

## 12. Return

You will return the Equipment to BIG on or before the Due Date, in the same condition as when you received it, ordinary wear excepted, complete with all cases, cables, batteries, media, and accessories. Return is not complete until BIG has received and accepted the Equipment.

## 13. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the Commonwealth of Massachusetts, without regard to its conflict-of-laws rules. You consent to the exclusive jurisdiction and venue of the state and federal courts located in Massachusetts for any dispute arising out of this Agreement.

## 14. Entire Agreement; Severability

This Agreement is the entire agreement between you and BIG regarding the Equipment and supersedes any prior understanding. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions will remain in full force and effect.

## 15. Electronic Signature

You agree that typing your name and submitting this Agreement constitutes your electronic signature under the federal E-SIGN Act and the Massachusetts Uniform Electronic Transactions Act, and has the same legal effect as a handwritten signature. You consent to receive and sign this Agreement electronically.
